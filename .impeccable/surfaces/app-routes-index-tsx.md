# Surface brief: WeedHub public site (home first, then directory, strain detail, and every public route)

Scope: replacement visual world for all public pages. Home = Persuade; directory/strain/guides/glossary = Read/Operate inside the same world.
Audience: curious adult in LATAM/Brazil arriving from search or AI answers, mostly mobile. es and pt equal.
Constraints: anonymous posting, 18+ framing, medical caution, no stock photos, no invented numbers.

## Direction contract

THESIS: The encyclopedia as a hand-lettered street wall — Mexican rótulos, Brazilian abridores de letras, fluorescent market cartulinas. Every strain is a sign the community keeps repainting. Refuses the dark-green cannabis database with neon accents and equal photo cards.

OWN-WORLD: Painted-wall fields own whole regions (cobalt #1d3fbf, plus wall colors per section), white enamel ground, black brush lettering with sign-painter drop shade on display type only. Strains are fluorescent cartulinas (pink, yellow, green, orange) lettered in marker, pinned slightly askew; THC lettered like a price. Controls are painted boards with distinct rest/hover/pressed/active/disabled states. Raises: evidence weight (review count sets how strong a rating reads; no data = "sin datos"), read/write one object (every strain sign carries "pinta tu reseña"), momento del día as a browse axis, distinct control states.

STORY: Visitor sees this is the cannabis encyclopedia in their language, searches immediately, recognizes strains as signs on a wall, opens one and gets a plain, cited answer, then trusts it enough to add their own.
